Frequently Asked Questions

General Coaching
Who is Erim's coaching designed for?

My coaching is tailored for ambitious professionals, corporate leaders, and entrepreneurs who want to elevate their professional presence. Whether you're an introvert looking to find your voice in meetings or a founder preparing for a global keynote, we provide the tools to help you lead with authority.

I'm naturally very introverted. Can I still be a great public speaker?

Absolutely. Some of the world's most impactful speakers are introverts. We don't try to change your personality — we refine your "performance persona." We focus on high-impact strategies that allow you to command a room without needing to be the loudest person in it.

What makes your coaching different from traditional media training?

Most media training focuses only on the "delivery." My approach integrates Sales, Networking, and Psychology. As someone who built a multi-million pound brand, I teach you how to speak in a way that actually converts — whether that's converting a lead into a sale or an audience into a community.

Networking & Professional Confidence
Can you help with "imposter syndrome" in professional settings?

Yes. This is a core pillar of my "Professional Confidence" module. We move beyond "fake it till you make it" and focus on science-backed techniques to eliminate low-confidence language (like over-apologising) and replace it with executive presence.

I struggle with networking events. Do you offer practical scripts?

I do! My networking coaching includes my "Beyond the Business Card" framework. You'll receive specific scripts for opening conversations, pivoting away from small talk, and the exact 24-hour follow-up sequence I used to secure partnerships with global brands.

Logistics & Booking
Do you offer 1-on-1 coaching or group programmes?

I offer both!

1-on-1 Coaching: Intensive, personalised sessions for high-stakes preparation. £50 deposit secures your slot, £100 balance after the session.
Masterclasses / Group Programmes: Collaborative environments focusing on networking and professional presence.
Corporate Workshops: Tailored training for teams and organisations.

How long does it take to see results?

While deep transformation takes time, most clients report a significant shift in their confidence and "vocal authority" after just one session. Packages are available when multiple sessions are booked in bulk to help with long-term growth and accountability.

Are sessions held in person or online?

To accommodate my global community, most coaching sessions are held via Google Meet (45–60 minutes). However, for keynote preparation or corporate workshops, in-person sessions in London can be arranged.
