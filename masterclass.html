The masterclass

Find Your Voice

A three-phase intensive — foundation, skill-set, transformation.

Phase 1

The Foundation

  • Arrival & refreshments. Settle in, grab a drink, meet your sisters.
  • The raw truth. We begin with Elevator Pitch #1 (Recorded). We start exactly where you are today — no judgment, just a baseline to see how far you'll go.
  • Unlearning the "quiet." Breaking the people-pleasing habit. Why we shrink in professional spaces — and how to reclaim that energy.
Phase 2

The Skill-Set

  • The "jobs" of fluency. Specific, actionable techniques to eliminate filler words and speak with authority in meetings and 1-to-1s.
  • Heritage as a superpower. A deep dive into using our cultural background and family-rooted narratives to command attention. We'll learn how to weave who we are into what we do.
  • Interactive workshop. Small-group practice. You'll be assigned specific "jobs" to practise one-on-one with your peers.
Phase 3

The Transformation

  • The final evolution. Elevator Pitch #2 (Recorded). This is the moment. You'll deliver your pitch in front of everyone, applying the frameworks and "jobs" we've mastered.
  • LinkedIn glow-up. A professional headshot session so your online presence matches your new offline confidence.
  • Closing & connections. Final thoughts and a chance to solidify those new friendships.
Checklist

What to bring

  • Pen & paper. You'll want to take notes on the specific frameworks I'll be sharing.
  • Your full self. Bring your confidence — even the shaky kind.
  • An open heart. We rise by lifting each other.
